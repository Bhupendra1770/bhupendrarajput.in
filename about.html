Who I Am

I'm Bhupendra Rajput — an AI Engineer, Solution Architect and Backend Tech Lead with 3.8 years of experience designing and shipping production-grade Generative AI systems. Originally from Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, I've worked across companies in Gurugram and beyond, building GenAI platforms that real users depend on.

My focus sits at the intersection of solution architecture and hands-on backend engineering: deciding how a system should be structured — RAG pipeline, MCP tool layer, multi-channel routing, database schema — and then writing the Python that makes it real.

I've architected multi-channel conversational agents spanning WhatsApp, phone, Telegram, email and web; built voice-enabled assistants with real-time STT/TTS; and led anomaly-detection and file-integrity pipelines for enterprise security. Outside client work, I build my own products, including an MCP-powered voice automation agent (Cue) and a self-serve print kiosk SaaS (PrintMe).

"I don't prototype — I ship."
